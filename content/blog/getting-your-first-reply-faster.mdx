Most job-search advice focuses on volume: apply to more roles, cast a wider net. But in our own usage data, the single biggest factor in getting a reply wasn't how many applications someone sent — it was how quickly they applied after a posting went live.

## Why speed matters

Recruiters often triage the first batch of applicants closely and skim the rest. If your tailored resume lands in the first hour, it's read with more attention than one that lands on day four, buried under three hundred others.

## What actually helps

- **Monitoring, not searching.** Checking job boards twice a day misses postings that open and fill within that window.
- **Tailoring that doesn't slow you down.** A resume tailored to the specific listing's language clears ATS keyword filters without costing you the speed advantage.
- **Submitting through the real flow.** Some shortcuts fill out a form but get flagged or dropped by the ATS — submitting through the system properly matters as much as submitting fast.

None of this requires more hours spent job hunting. It requires catching postings earlier and reacting to them faster, which is exactly the part that's easiest to automate.
